Epoxy Flooring

Floors That Impress.

From raw concrete to a showroom-quality surface in a single day. Our industrial-grade epoxy systems are engineered for both beauty and extreme durability — perfect for residential garages, commercial spaces, and everything in between.

All installations include surface preparation, primer, colour/flake coat, and a UV-stable topcoat — backed by our exclusive 3-year warranty.

  • Garage floor flake & chip coatings
  • Metallic & marble swirl epoxy (decorative interiors)
  • Basement & utility floor coatings
  • Commercial & industrial floor systems
  • Exterior patio & deck coatings
  • Diamond grinding, crack repair & moisture treatment

Smart Door Locks

Keys Are Optional.

Upgrade your home or business with the latest smart lock technology. We supply, install, configure, and test all major brands — leaving you with a fully working system and the app ready to go.

Keypad Door Locks

  • Multiple unique PIN codes for family members
  • Temporary guest codes that expire automatically
  • Auto-lock after a configurable time period
  • Low-battery alerts and battery backup included

Fingerprint Biometric Locks

  • Stores up to 100 fingerprint profiles
  • Unlocks in under 0.5 seconds in all weather
  • Backup PIN and physical key options
  • Access log — see who entered and when

Wi-Fi & App-Controlled Locks

  • Remote lock / unlock from anywhere in the world
  • Real-time push notifications on every entry
  • Works with Alexa, Google Home, Apple HomeKit
  • Full activity log in the app

Smart Deadbolt Retrofit

  • Fits most standard doors — no exterior hardware change
  • Installs in under 30 minutes
